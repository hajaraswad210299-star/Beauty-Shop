Navigating the world of serums

Serums have become the quiet heroes of the modern skincare shelf. Concentrated, lightweight and designed to deliver active ingredients deep where they’re needed most, they bridge the gap between cleansing and moisturising with remarkable precision.

But with endless options lining the shelves, choosing the right one can feel overwhelming. The key is to start with your skin’s needs rather than the label’s promises — hydration, brightening, firming or calming — and to build slowly from there.

“A serum is not about doing more. It’s about doing exactly what your skin is asking for, and nothing it isn’t.”

Know your skin first

Before reaching for a bottle, spend a week simply observing. Is your skin tight after cleansing, or shiny by midday? Does it flush easily, or stay calm? These small signals tell you far more than any trend, and they point you toward the textures and actives that will actually serve you.

Once you understand your skin, layering becomes intuitive. Apply serums from thinnest to thickest, give each a moment to absorb, and always seal everything with a moisturiser and — in the morning — sunscreen. Consistency, not intensity, is what delivers the glow.

Give any new formula a fair trial of at least four weeks. Skin renews on its own quiet schedule, and the most meaningful results are the ones that arrive gently, then stay.
